Mitya as a support

Mitya is played both as a support and as a DPS, about equally. What he gives the party:

  • Elemental Mastery: with a Steady State Detection Core, every party member gains 30 Elemental Mastery for each Gradient Beacon Mitya has. His Elemental Burst creates 3 beacons.
  • Stellar-Conduct: every point of Elemental Mastery he has raises the party's Base Stellar-Conduct DMG by 0.028%, up to 14% at 500 Elemental Mastery.

So a support Mitya wants Elemental Mastery, about 500 for the full Stellar-Conduct bonus, plus enough Energy Recharge for his 60-Energy burst.

How this was calculated

  • DPS build. Situation: Overloaded Detection Core, in Radiance: Stellar-Conduct, consuming Gradient Beacons with Gradient Detonation. Maximized: one Gradient Beacon's Stellar-Conduct hit from Gradient Detonation (his special Charged Attack), as an average hit (CRIT Rate × CRIT DMG).
  • Level 90, talents 9/9/9, constellation 0.
  • Artifacts: 5★ +20 with KQM Standards substats: 2 rolls of every substat plus 20 more placed by the optimizer, at most 2 per artifact and none on a piece whose main stat is the same stat.
  • Weapons: level 90, 5★ at refinement 1 and 4★ at refinement 5.
  • Enemy: level 100 with 10% RES.
  • Solo, no team buffs. Set and weapon effects the character can trigger alone are at full stacks; effects that need teammates, shields, healing or taking damage are off, and effects that rotate are averaged.
  • No Energy Recharge requirement: check that your build has enough to use the Elemental Burst on rotation.
  • Computed on 2026-11-09.
