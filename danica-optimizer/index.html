Danica as a support

Danica is mainly played as a support, in Defense Maid. What she gives the party, at talent level 9:

  • Party ATK: after her Elemental Burst in Radiance: Stellar-Conduct, Defense Maid gives every party member ATK equal to 11.9% of Danica's own ATK, up to 475. That cap needs about 4,000 ATK, so in practice every point of her ATK counts.
  • Stellar-Conduct: every 100 ATK she has raises the party's Base Stellar-Conduct DMG by 0.7%, up to 14% at 2,000 ATK.
  • RES shred: her Anchor lowers enemies' Electro RES by 15%, and in Radiance by another 15% plus 30% Cryo RES, whatever her stats.

So a support Danica wants ATK: ATK% main stats (or an Energy Recharge sands if she needs one), ATK% substats and a weapon with high base ATK, plus enough Energy Recharge for her 60-Energy burst. In the app, turn on Defense Maid and the burst enhancement to see the exact ATK she gives each teammate.

How this was calculated

  • DPS build. Situation: Battle Maid on-field, in Radiance: Stellar-Conduct, after her Elemental Burst. Maximized: On-Field Skill E2 damage (Stellar-Conduct), as an average hit (CRIT Rate × CRIT DMG).
  • Level 90, talents 9/9/9, constellation 0.
  • Artifacts: 5★ +20 with KQM Standards substats: 2 rolls of every substat plus 20 more placed by the optimizer, at most 2 per artifact and none on a piece whose main stat is the same stat.
  • Weapons: level 90, 5★ at refinement 1 and 4★ at refinement 5.
  • Enemy: level 100 with 10% RES.
  • Solo, no team buffs. Set and weapon effects the character can trigger alone are at full stacks; effects that need teammates, shields, healing or taking damage are off, and effects that rotate are averaged.
  • No Energy Recharge requirement: check that your build has enough to use the Elemental Burst on rotation.
  • Danica isn't released yet: these numbers use the beta data of 2026-11-06 and can change before release.
