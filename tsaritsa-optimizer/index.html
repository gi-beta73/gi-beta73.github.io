The Tsaritsa as a support

The Tsaritsa is played both as a support and as a DPS, about equally, in Stellar-Conduct and Stellar Swirl teams. What she gives the party, at talent level 9:

  • CRIT DMG: her Elemental Skill gives every party member Accolades for 20s: +12% CRIT DMG.
  • Stellar Glimmer DMG: with the Blessing of Love, her Elemental Burst To the New World increases nearby party members' Stellar Glimmer reaction DMG (Stellar-Conduct and Stellar Swirl) by 47.5%.
  • Base DMG: every 100 ATK she has raises the party's Base Stellar-Conduct and Stellar Swirl DMG by 0.7%, up to 14% at 2,000 ATK.
  • Stellar Swirl: while she is in the party, Stellar Swirl's base reaction multiplier is 15% higher.

So a support Tsaritsa wants ATK, about 2,000 for the full base DMG bonus, plus enough Energy Recharge for her 50-Energy burst.

How this was calculated

  • Stellar-Conduct team: DPS build. Situation: Blessing of Honor on-field, in Radiance: Stellar-Conduct, during The Evernight Yields to Dawn. Maximized: one full Honor Normal Attack chain, including its Stellar-Conduct hits, as an average hit (CRIT Rate × CRIT DMG).
  • Stellar Swirl team: DPS build. Situation: Blessing of Honor on-field, in Radiance: Stellar Swirl, during The Evernight Yields to Dawn. Maximized: one full Honor Normal Attack chain, including its Stellar Swirl hits, as an average hit (CRIT Rate × CRIT DMG).
  • Level 90, talents 9/9/9, constellation 0.
  • Weapons whose passive counts party members (marked in the weapon table) are ranked with three teammates of other elements.
  • Artifacts: 5★ +20 with KQM Standards substats: 2 rolls of every substat plus 20 more placed by the optimizer, at most 2 per artifact and none on a piece whose main stat is the same stat.
  • Weapons: level 90, 5★ at refinement 1 and 4★ at refinement 5.
  • Enemy: level 100 with 10% RES.
  • Solo, no team buffs. Set and weapon effects the character can trigger alone are at full stacks; effects that need teammates, shields, healing or taking damage are off, and effects that rotate are averaged.
  • No Energy Recharge requirement: check that your build has enough to use the Elemental Burst on rotation.
  • The Tsaritsa isn't released yet: these numbers use the beta data of 2026-11-06 and can change before release.
